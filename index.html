House rules

Know your flags

Blue flag

A faster car is behind you. Hold your line and let them through. It's a race night, not a road block.

Yellow flag

Someone's off ahead. No passing until you're clear of the incident. The steward is watching the replay.

Chequered

Race over. Shake hands, hand the rig back, and buy the winner a drink if you punted them in turn one.
